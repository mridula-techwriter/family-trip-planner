## Product Manager

- **Plan:** Fork the group app and change only what family use needs: two roles (Owner, Child), Budget replaces split/settle up, and a new "Before you go" area (documents, home & cats). Ship in four small releases, separation first.
- **Why:** Mridula wants no redundant effort, and the group app already solves itinerary, bookings, tickets, reminders, packing, PDFs and offline. Family apps reviewed (Wanderlog, TripIt) are built for general audiences; Wanderlog's family appeal is sharing, a budget and multi-currency expenses, which matches the three changes. Data law: the DPDP Act does not apply to data an individual processes for a personal or domestic purpose (s.3(c)(i)), so a private family planner holding the child's details is outside it. Not legal advice; it would change if the app were ever offered to other families.
- **Risks or concerns:** Scope creep. Four extras plus a fork is a lot; documents is the most sensitive and goes last.
- **Need from Mridula:** Answers to the open questions below, and the date of the next family trip (it sets the deadline).

## UX Designer

- **Plan:** Keep the "My travel plans" home. Inside a trip, group tabs into three: **Plan** (itinerary, bookings, tickets), **Money** (budget), **Before you go** (packing, to-dos, documents check, home & cats). The child sees a simpler app: **Today** and **My packing** only.
- **Why:** The group app has eight tabs; adding three more would crowd a 412px screen. Mridula forgets where things are, so fewer, named places beat more tabs. Home & cats details barely change between trips, so they are set up once and reused (copy into each trip).
- **Risks or concerns:** Disagrees with the Developer on offline copies of documents (see Discussion). Itinerary gets an optional **Break** item (rest, meal, nap) so a child-paced day is visible.
- **Need from Mridula:** Pick a name and palette from the options below.

## UX Writer

- **Plan:** Reuse the group app's terms exactly ("My travel plans", "Create a travel plan", "Mark as complete") so both apps feel the same in the hand. New terms: **Budget**, **Spent**, **Left**. Child-facing words written for a child: "Things to pack", "Today".
- **Why:** One name per thing across Mridula's apps cuts relearning. Budget wording leads with the answer: "₹8,400 left of ₹60,000".
- **Risks or concerns:** Passport warnings must say the consequence plainly: "Passport expires Mon 3 May 2027, less than 6 months after you return. Some countries won't let you in." Many countries require 6 months' validity beyond the travel date (Nepal and Bhutan are exceptions), and the rule is set by the destination, not India.
- **Need from Mridula:** Nothing now.

## Product Developer

- **Plan:** Copy `index.html`, `sw.js`, manifest and icons into this repo; serve from GitHub Pages at `mridula-techwriter.github.io/family-trip-planner/`. New Apps Script project, new Google Sheet, new private Drive folder, new passcodes. Currency rates from Frankfurter (free, no key), auto-filled and editable.
- **Why:** Same stack as the group app: free, no accounts, already proven on Mridula's phone.
- **Risks or concerns (important):** Both apps live on the same web address (`mridula-techwriter.github.io`), so on one phone they **share browser storage and the offline cache**. Unchanged, the family app would read the group app's saved passcode and role (`tripRole`, `curTrip`, `tripData.v1`) and the two service workers would delete each other's caches (`trip-shell-vN`). Fix in release 1: prefix every stored key (`fam.`), rename the cache (`family-shell-vN`), and only delete own caches. Second risk: the group app's `Code.gs` isn't in GitHub; I need a copy to reuse it.
- **Need from Mridula:** A copy of the group app's `Code.gs` (or permission to write a fresh one from the README spec), then about 10 minutes to create the new Sheet and deploy the new backend with my click-by-click steps.

## Discussion

- **Offline copies of documents.** Designer: copies of passports on the phone help at a counter with no signal. Developer: offline copies sit in browser storage on a phone the child may also use, and the child's link must never see them. Trade-off: convenience vs exposure. Team recommends expiry dates only first, copies later and online-only for the Owner.
- **Exchange rate.** Developer wanted manual rates only (simpler); PM and Writer want auto-fill because Mridula won't look rates up mid-trip. Agreed: auto-fill the day's rate, editable, because card and forex-counter rates differ; save the original amount, rate and ₹ amount.

## Decisions

| Decision | Why |
|---|---|
| Fork the group app; separate repo, Pages site, Apps Script, Sheet, Drive folder and passcodes | Mridula: completely separate; no redundant effort |
| Namespace browser storage and offline cache | Same web address as the group app; prevents passcode and cache clashes |
| Roles: Owner (Mridula, everything) and Child (own link) | Mridula + child use it |
| Budget vs spent replaces split / settle up | Mridula's choice |
| Expenses in any currency, converted to ₹ | Trips abroad |
| Home & cats set up once, copied into each trip | Details rarely change |
| New name and colours | Never open the wrong app |
